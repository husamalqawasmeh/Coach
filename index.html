Meet your coach

Coach Fahmy

Coach Fahmy is a personal trainer focused on corrective exercise and movement quality. His approach is simple: find the root cause of pain, poor posture, or plateaus, fix it, and then progress you safely toward your goals.

Whether you sit at a desk all day, are coming back from an injury, or want to perform at your best, every program starts with a full posture and movement assessment, so your training is built for your body.

  • Posture, movement & mobility assessments
  • Programs tailored to muscle imbalances and compensations
  • Strength, fat loss & athletic performance coaching
  • Progress tracking with regular re-assessments
The method

Corrective first, then stronger

Every session follows a proven corrective exercise sequence before load is added.

01

Release

Foam rolling and soft-tissue work to calm overactive muscles.

02

Lengthen

Targeted stretching to restore range of motion.

03

Activate

Wake up underactive muscles that should be doing the work.

04

Integrate

Full-body movements that lock in the new pattern under load.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need any experience?

No. Every program starts with an assessment and is scaled to your current level.

What happens in the free assessment?

A 30-minute check of your posture, movement and mobility, plus a chat about your goals. You leave with clear next steps, no obligation.

Can I train online?

Yes. Online clients get video sessions, a training app plan and weekly check-ins.

I have pain or a past injury. Can you help?

Corrective exercise is built for this. If anything needs medical attention, we'll refer you to a doctor or physiotherapist first.

How do I pay?

Contact us to book. Packages are paid up front; monthly plans are billed each month.
